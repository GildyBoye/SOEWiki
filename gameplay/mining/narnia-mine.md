* **Requirement:** [Gilion's Quest](gilions-quests.md) (Mission 3) and [Vaccine Quest](../../exploration/access-quests/vaccine-quest-narnia.md)
* **Veins:** Coal, Adamant and Runite veins
* **Ticket Fee:** 50,000 GP
* **Ticket duration:** 1h
