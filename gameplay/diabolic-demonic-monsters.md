# Diabolic/Demonic Monsters

### Diabolics/Demonics on Souls of Elysium <a href="#diabolics_demonics_on_souls_of_elysium" id="diabolics_demonics_on_souls_of_elysium"></a>

**Souls of Elysium** offers new and engaging mini-boss system, you will face many enemies who after death or during natural spawn can turn into stronger versions of themselves with additional abilities, health and damage.

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (527).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (529).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* There are two tiers: **Demonic** (white skull) and the stronger **Diabolic** (red skull).
* Around a 1% chance to spawn naturally or after a kill.
* Usually they have around 2-6x Health and Experience of the basic version of the monster, with Demonics at the lower end and Diabolics at the higher end.
* Both tiers have boosted loot tables, and access to unique drops, making them quite rewarding.
* Additionally players with Premium Account will be able to gain [skull coins](skull-coins-hourly-tokens/) for each diabolic kill.
* Extremely dangerous, never take these for granted as a few of them can send you on a quick trip to the temple.

{% hint style="info" %}
Most promoted monsters share a common loot pool on top of their normal loot:

* **Demonic:** Platinum Coins, [Scroll of Identification](upgrading-and-rarity.md), Upgrade Catalyst, Crystal Extractor, Medium Experience Boost, Gem Box, [Item Box](skull-coins-hourly-tokens/item-box.md), Demonic Essence and [Clue Scroll (Easy)](clue-scrolls.md).
* **Diabolic:** Star Ring, Scroll of Identification, Platinum Coins, Gem Box, Upgrade Catalyst, Crystal Extractor, Item Box, Medium Experience Boost and Demonic Essence.
{% endhint %}
