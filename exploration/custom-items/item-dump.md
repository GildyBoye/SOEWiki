# Item Browser

* Covers every wearable item: helmets, armors, legs, boots, shields, weapons, ammo, amulets, rings, backpacks and pets.
* Click a column to sort (Armor, Attack, Magic Level, Weight...) and click it again to reverse.
* Search matches item names and bonuses, e.g. `magic level`, `fire`, `crit`.
* Use the protection filter to find gear that protects against a specific element.
