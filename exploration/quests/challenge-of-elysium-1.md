# Challenge of Elysium 1

**Level Required:** 40+ in a Team

**Rewards:** 1 Gem Box, 4 Gold Ingots

**Location:** Darashia Minotaur Pyramid, Bottom Floor

**Monsters:** Enraged versions of Minotaurs, Dwarfs, Dragons, Beholders, Lizards.

***

* Head to the bottom of the Darashia Minotaur Pyramid and enter the teleport.
* After this point, when you enter this teleport you won’t be able to come back, the only way to leave is to complete the quest, or die.
